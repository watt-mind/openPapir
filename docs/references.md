# References and evidence policy

This repository defines project boundaries; it does not contain a government
service specification or claim conformance with any receipt or KRX profile.

## Related independent projects

- [openKRX](https://github.com/watt-mind/openKRX): proposed KRX container layer.
- [openSzigno](https://github.com/watt-mind/openSzigno): `.es3` dossier tooling
  and the reference for this repository's engineering quality baseline.

## Discovery entry points

- [Official e-Papír help](https://epapir.gov.hu/sugo).
- [Official police NOVA.PACK information][novapack].

[novapack]: https://ugyintezes.police.hu/novapack

These are research entry points, not adopted implementation contracts. Police
integration documentation must not be treated as general e-Papír API access.

For each future format or integration decision, record the issuing organisation,
document title, version/date, exact URL and relevant section, retrieval date,
licence/redistribution terms, and the specific claim supported. Distinguish
normative requirements from examples and observed producer behaviour.

Publicly accessible documentation or schemas are not automatically licensed
for redistribution. Link sources until their terms are established, and keep
linking where the established terms reserve all rights. Use original code and
synthetic fixtures, and keep uncertain requirements visible in the
[roadmap](roadmap.md).

Record the route a retrieval took, not only its outcome. A source that a
direct fetch cannot reach may be perfectly public by another route, and a
route that failed once is evidence about access rather than about content.
